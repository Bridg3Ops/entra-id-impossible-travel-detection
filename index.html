THE DETECTION

Geography opens
the investigation.

Successful interactive sign-ins from two or more countries within one hour surface an account for review. A VPN, proxy or mobile network can produce the same signal as suspicious activity.

This is geographic diversity, not an impossible-travel model. It does not calculate distance, travel speed or a learned baseline.

Collect

Read Entra ID SigninLogs over a rolling one-hour window.

Qualify

Keep successful interactive events with a resolved country and account. Apply optional exact exclusions.

Aggregate

Group by normalised account. Return at least two distinct countries, with IP and application context.

Investigate

Inspect the source events and authentication details. Determine whether simulation, benign routing or misuse explains the signal.

ANALYST WORKFLOW

Build the evidence chain.

Establish the facts

Confirm the query version, UTC window, contributing events and sign-in outcomes. Aggregated rows do not preserve the full sequence.

Test explanations

Review VPN scope, device continuity, authentication steps, Conditional Access and related alerts. Record gaps instead of filling them with assumptions.

Make a bounded decision

Classify the case and document the rationale. Geography alone does not justify a compromise finding or automatic containment.

Coverage boundaries

Failed and non-interactive sign-ins, service identities, unresolved geography, same-country misuse and events outside the time window are outside this query's coverage. Late ingestion can create gaps. A successful sign-in does not prove downstream access or malicious activity.
